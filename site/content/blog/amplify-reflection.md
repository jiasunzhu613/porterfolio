Through a series of miraculous strokes of luck, from being rejected to a consolation acceptance few weeks later, I ended up interning at RBC in the Amplify program this summer.

To start, I am incredibly grateful for what this summer has turned out to be. The work I did, the knowledge I learned, the mistakes I made, the people I met and the experiences I have encountered were so formative for me as not just an engineer but as a human being. In all honesty, RBC Amplify **really** isn't your typical internship. 

## A Letter To Your Future Self

As part of Amplify, during the start of the summer, I wrote a letter to my future self. 

> To the dumbass writing this,
> 
> Stay stupid. I don't mean this because you are not capable, not because you are inept at learning, not because you can't grow but because you should take this opportunity to reset. Learn from everyone around you; your student peers, your mentors, the RBC Amplify program staff and the executives. Get rid of your preconceived notions of right and wrong and take everything in to the fullest. After all, your world view is the most important thing to your but it is valuable to be open-minded!
> 
> I want you to use this opportunity to learn with new people and develop more lateral skills like communication/soft skills. You will feel times of embarrassment, times of ridicule but these are parts of the fire that will forge you into a blade from metal! Embrace embarrassment, embrace ridicule, most of all embrace stupidity and work your hardest!
>  
>  Spend time with your family though. Nothing is more important in this world than them.
>  
>  Regardless of the outcome I want you to learn, develop and reflect that your time was not wasted and the experience was at least worth it. Regardless if you win or lose, or do anything great, still stay stupid and continue to learn!
