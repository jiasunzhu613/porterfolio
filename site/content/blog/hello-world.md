I have always wanted to write, but never really put any effort towards doing so. This will be my first real attempt at blogging and documenting what I am up to, whether that is tech related or just random snippets of my life.

I have always looked up to people who write, formally or informally, and I have grew tremendously from reading the things other people have written. Particularly, [Max Bernstein](https://bernsteinbear.com/), [Michal Pitr](https://michalpitr.substack.com/), [Patrick Collison](https://patrickcollison.com/) and so many more people. To those who tune into the subsequent blogs, I hope they can help provide even the slightest insight into something interesting you didn't know about and make you a fuller person.
